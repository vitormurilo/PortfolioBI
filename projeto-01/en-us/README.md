# Sales and Margin in Retail

English edition of the two-page PortfolioBI report published in commit `c33d8bd`. Executive Overview shows net sales, orders, estimated gross profit and the prior-year comparison. Products and Margin shows product rankings and a category and product breakdown.

Open [Sales and Margin.pbip](Sales%20and%20Margin.pbip) in Power BI Desktop. To refresh on another computer, set `SalesWorkbookPath` under **Transform data → Manage parameters** to the full path of [Sales_Data_USD.xlsx](Sales_Data_USD.xlsx), apply the change and select **Refresh**.

The workbook contains 1,602 synthetic sales lines from 2025 and 2026. Prices, discounts, net sales and reference costs were converted using a fixed illustrative rate of BRL 5.00 per USD 1.00. Source precision is retained while displayed currency values use two decimal places. Estimated gross profit subtracts reference costs from net sales.
